The hub city

Chester, Pennsylvania

A majority-Black city on the Delaware River hosts Reworld Delaware Valley, formerly Covanta, burning up to 3,500 tons of waste per day. Only a tiny fraction of the trash burned here is Chester's own.

FacilityReworld Delaware Valley, operating since 1992
ScaleRoughly 1.2 million tons of waste burned per year
